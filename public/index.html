Computed live from the Materials and Crew/Contractor entries under each Sequence of Events stage, combined into one figure per stage. Committed = every planned material amount + all crew allocations at Planned Qty; Actual = consumed material amount + crew cost at Actual Qty. Budget is the Approved Budget (Project Brief) apportioned across stages in proportion to their share of Committed cost — so the Budget column always sums back to the Approved Budget. Variance = Budget − Actual (green = under budget, red = over).
